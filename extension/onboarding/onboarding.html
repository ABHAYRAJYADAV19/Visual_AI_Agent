Here's exactly what this extension can collect

Transparency is our core principle. Nothing is collected without your explicit permission. Here's a plain-language breakdown:

🖱️

Activity Events

Click positions, scroll depth, page navigations, and tab switches. We capture where you click (coordinates and element type), never what you type.

✓ No text content captured ✓ No form values read
📸

Visual Capture (Screenshots)

Periodic screenshots of your active tab, analyzed by AI to categorize your activity type (e.g., "browsing documentation", "video call"). The AI describes activity categories, never transcribes visible text.

⚡ Separate opt-in required ✓ No text transcription
🔒

What We NEVER Collect

  • Password field values
  • Credit card or payment information
  • Social security numbers or government IDs
  • Form inputs containing personal data
  • Data from domains you've blocked
